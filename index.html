FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Queries About Our Services

How Do I Get An Estimate For My Move?
You can get a free estimate by calling our helpline or filling out the quick enquiry form at the top of the page.
How Far In Advance Should I Book My Relocation?
We recommend booking at least 1-2 weeks in advance to ensure smooth scheduling and availability.
How Do You Ensure The Safety Of Fragile Items?
We use multi-layer premium packing materials like bubble wrap, foam peanuts, and specialized cartons for delicate items.
Do You Provide Insurance For My Belongings?
Yes, we offer comprehensive transit insurance to protect your goods against any unforeseen circumstances during the move.
What Is Included In Your "Lifestyle & Maintenance" Services?
We Go Beyond Shifting To Help You Settle In. This Includes Pre-Verified Hotel Bookings, Car Rentals, And Professional Maintenance Like Plumbing, Electrical Work, And Car Painting. You Can Book These As Standalone Services Or As Part Of Your Moving Package.
Can I Track My Shipment?
Absolutely! We provide a dedicated tracking ID so you can monitor the status of your moving truck in real-time.
